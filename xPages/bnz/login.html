Update your BNZ app to keep using two-step authentication

To keep using two-step authentication for Internet Banking, update your app to the latest version. On your phone, open the App Store or Google Play Store, search for BNZ Mobile, then select Update.

Your app needs to be on Apple version 18.7.1 or later, or Android version 9.55 or later.
